# Business rules

The planning rules the app calculates with. They are documented here and are **not shown in the UI**.
The app applies them silently and only surfaces something when it affects the decision in front of the
user: a missing or uncertain value, or a conflict with the role's dates.

Each rule is marked by where it came from:

- **Discovery call**: told to us by the client.
- **Our decision**: our own call, where the brief or data was silent.

The full reasoning and trade-offs are in [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md).

## What the UI deliberately does not show

- Labels saying whether a rule is ours or came from the client.
- Assignment or time-off record IDs. Role IDs like R04 are shown, because they work as request references.
- Explanations of how a number is calculated, beyond the short definitions of Capacity, Committed,
  Proposed, Free and Utilization in the week-by-week view.

Uncertainty that could change a staffing decision is always shown where the decision is made:
inferred end dates, requested (unapproved) time off, missing skills, tentative work, and synthetic
"demo data" fields.
